Reactor Designs and Innovations

Compared to other energy sources, nuclear energy stands apart due to its technological complexity, which results in a longer and more structured development process.

Only a small number of concepts reach the prototype stage, and even fewer become commercially deployed reactors.

Explore the Reactor Encyclopedia to discover how these technologies work, how they evolved, and how far each has progressed from concept to deployment.
